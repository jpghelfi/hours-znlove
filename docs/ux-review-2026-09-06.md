# UX review — navigation and page chrome (2026-09-06)

Scope: the menu, the header of every page, duplicated features, and how it all
holds up on a phone. Reviewed on the live templates at 1280px and 390px.
What was shipped from this review is in `docs/navigation.md`; the rest is a
backlog, ordered by value.

## What was wrong

**The menu.** Nine flat links for an admin, plus name, Log out and a theme
button. At 1280px the bar already wrapped to two rows (90px, sticky). At phone
width it wrapped to three ragged rows inside a sticky header: a third of the
screen gone on every page, and the tap targets were 13px text. Nothing was
grouped, so "Reports / By project / Budgets / Assignments / Invoices" read as
five equal things when three of them are the same filter row over the same
entries.

**Five ways to say "move in time."** Week: `← Prev / This week / Next →`.
Absences: `← Earlier / This week / Later →`. Schedule: `← Earlier / Now /
Later →`. Reports: four range chips + From/To + Apply. By project and
Budgets: `← / This month / →`. Same gesture, five vocabularies; on a phone the
three-word versions wrapped to two rows.

**Destructive next to navigational.** On Schedule, 🗑 Clear week sat in the
same button row as the period arrows.

**Log out as a first-class item.** A once-a-month action with a permanent
slot; the theme toggle likewise.

**Absences led with the form.** The log form was the whole first screen; the
"who's away" dashboard, which is why most people open the page, was below the
fold.

**Three export buttons on `/project`.** CSV, Excel, and Export (which also
downloads Excel).

## Duplicates and overlaps

| Pair | What overlaps | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Log hours vs My week | Two ways to write the same Time Entry. The form has the timer, ticket link and description; the grid is bulk. | Same job, two views. Kept both, but grouped under *Track* so they read as siblings. A later step: one **Time** page with a form/grid toggle. |
| Reports vs By project | Same filter row (people, PM, AM, projects, partner), same entries, both export CSV. Reports is person-first over a date *range*; By project is project-first over one *period* and carries goals, Excel, the export screen and invoices. | Grouped under *Reports ▾* with one-line descriptions. Full merge (one Reports page with a People/Projects tab and one period control) is the biggest remaining win and the biggest change. |
| Budgets vs By project | Budgets is "tracked hours per project this month" with an allowance column; By project (monthly) is the same numbers without the allowance. | Grouped under *Reports ▾*. Could become a column toggle on By project. |
| Schedule vs Assignments | Booking someone on Schedule silently adds them to the project's People. Assignments is the same membership as a checkbox grid, plus PM/AM/partner. | Different jobs (plan vs configure). Assignments is really *project settings* and now sits under *Admin*. |
| Reports for non-admins | The route redirects non-admins to `/`, yet `_report_data` supports a `scope=me` view. | Either expose "My reports" to everyone or delete the me-scope code. Not touched. |
| Invoices header | Two project pickers in one row: the list filter and the New-invoice picker. | Not touched; move New invoice into a dialog. |

## What shipped

1. **Grouped desktop menu**: Log hours · My week · Absences · Schedule ·
   Reports ▾ (Reports, By project, Budgets) · Admin ▾ (Invoices,
   Assignments) · avatar menu (name, theme, log out). One 54px row.
2. **Phone tab bar** at the bottom: Log · Week · Schedule · Reports (or
   Absences for non-admins) · More, with a bottom sheet listing everything by
   group. Top bar is brand + avatar only.
3. **One period vocabulary** everywhere: `←` · label · `→`, with
   `aria-label`s, laid out narrow-wide-narrow on a phone.
4. **Schedule bulk actions** in their own group, apart from the arrows.
5. **`/project` downloads** folded into one ⬇ Download ▾ menu.
6. **Absences** form folded; dashboard leads; the form reopens itself after
   a save or a refusal.
7. Labels: "Weekly grid" → "My week".

## Backlog, in order

1. **Merge Reports and By project** into one Reports page with a
   People / Projects tab and a single period control (`_period_range` plus a
   custom range). Removes the duplicate filter row and the two CSV routes'
   drift risk.
2. **Reports filter row on a phone**: nine full-width controls before any
   number. Fold the pickers into a "Filters (2)" disclosure on ≤ 560px, like
   the More sheet; keep the range chips visible.
3. **Invoices header**: one project picker; "＋ New invoice" opens a dialog.
4. **Log hours on a phone**: the description hint wraps to two lines and the
   ticket placeholder truncates; shorten both. The timer button could sit in
   the Hours field's row.
5. **My week on a phone**: the capacity line ("8 / 40h this week") clips at
   the right edge; the grid's 520px floor means Thursday and Friday are
   off-screen. Consider a day-at-a-time layout under 560px, like `/project`
   already does with its table.
6. **Non-admin Reports**: expose or delete (see table).
7. **Tokens**: emoji icons (👥 📁 🧭 🤝) render differently per platform and
   read heavier on Windows; the tab bar now uses stroke SVGs and the filter
   pickers could follow.
